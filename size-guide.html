📿

Measure around the narrowest part of your wrist

Method 1: Using a Measuring Tape

  1. Wrap a soft measuring tape around your wrist
  2. Position it just below the wrist bone
  3. Keep the tape snug but not tight
  4. Note the measurement in inches or centimeters
  5. Add 0.5" (1.3cm) for a comfortable fit

Method 2: Using a String

  1. Wrap a piece of string around your wrist
  2. Mark where the string overlaps
  3. Lay the string flat and measure with a ruler
  4. Note the measurement
  5. Add 0.5" (1.3cm) for a comfortable fit

Choose Your Fit

Different people prefer different bracelet fits. Consider your preference:

Snug Fit

Add 0.3" - 0.5" to wrist measurement
Bracelet stays in place, minimal movement

Comfort Fit

Add 0.5" - 0.7" to wrist measurement
Recommended for most wearers

Loose Fit

Add 0.7" - 1.0" to wrist measurement
More movement, stacking friendly

Special Considerations

For Men

Most men wear size L (7.0" - 7.5"). If you prefer a looser fit or have a larger wrist, choose XL. Our Pixiu and Tiger Eye bracelets are particularly popular among male customers.

For Women

Most women wear size M (6.5" - 7.0"). Our Jade, Rose Quartz, and Amethyst bracelets are available in smaller sizes (XS, S) for petite wrists.

For Stacking

If you plan to wear multiple bracelets together, consider choosing one size larger for a more comfortable stacked look.

Bead Size Matters

Larger beads (10mm+) will fit more snugly than smaller beads (6-8mm) of the same bracelet length. If you prefer larger beads, consider sizing up.
